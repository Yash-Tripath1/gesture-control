🖐 Gesture Guide

☝️
Move Cursor
Point with index finger — cursor follows your fingertip
👌
Click
Pinch index + thumb together (left hand = left click)
✌️
Scroll Up / Down
Pinch with right hand, move hand up/down to scroll
🤙
Right Click
Pinch index + thumb with right hand (brief pinch)
🤏
Drag
Pinch and hold while moving your hand
